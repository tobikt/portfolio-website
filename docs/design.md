# Design & Styling Requirements

## Aesthetic
- **Style**: Professional, minimalist, and modern.
- **Visual Language**: Clean lines, ample whitespace, consistent spacing.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: Deep dark tones (e.g., `#0a0a0a`, `#121212`).
- **Text**: High contrast (e.g., `#e0e0e0` for body, `#ffffff` for headings).
- **Accent**: A single cohesive accent color (e.g., electric blue or soft purple) for CTAs, links, and highlights.

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or system-ui) for a contemporary and highly readable look.
- **Hierarchy**: Clear distinction between headings, sub-headings, and body text through weight and size.

## Responsiveness
- **Strategy**: Mobile-first.
- **Breakpoints**: Smooth transitions between Mobile (< 640px), Tablet (640px - 1024px), and Desktop (> 1024px).

## Interactivity & UX
- **Micro-interactions**: Subtle hover effects on buttons and links, smooth scroll behavior.
- **Loading States**: Skeleton screens or subtle spinners for dynamic content.
- **Navigation**: Intuitive and accessible navigation (e.g., sticky header on desktop, hamburger menu on mobile).
